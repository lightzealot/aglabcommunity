title: A template to create images in Google Flow
summary: Define the subject, scene, style, lighting and framing of each image.
---
## Formula

[Subject] + [Scene] + [Visual style] + [Lighting] + [Framing]

## Full template

```text
Create an image of [SUBJECT] in [SCENE]. Use a [VISUAL STYLE] style, with [TYPE OF LIGHT] lighting and [SHOT AND ANGLE] framing. The image will be used for [FINAL USE]. Keep [IMPORTANT COLORS, TEXTURES OR DETAILS].
```

## Example

A bottle of cold brew on a stone table, product photography, warm side light, close-up, blurred background. The image will be used as an Instagram ad. Keep a brown, cream and gold palette.

## Quick review

1. Is the subject clearly defined?
2. Does the scene have context?
3. Does the style match the result you want?
4. Does the light have a direction or intent?
5. Does the framing state the shot and angle?

Note: access to Google Flow and its generations depend on each account's availability, plan and credits.
