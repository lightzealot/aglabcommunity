Does your AI voice sound like a robot? It's not your fault: most people use the default voice. These **3 tools** make voices that sound real, and I made the voice in the reel with one of them. Here is the step-by-step for each one, free to start.

## The 3, in a table

| Tool | What it does | Free? |
|---|---|---|
| **MiniMax Audio** | Generates voices, clones yours and makes music. It's from MiniMax, the company behind Hailuo. | Free trials when you sign up, with limits. |
| **Google AI Studio** (Gemini voice) | You describe the style, pace and tone in plain words. | Free to try in AI Studio, with limits. The voice model is in preview. |
| **ElevenLabs** | Very realistic voices, a voice library and cloning. It's the one I use. | Free plan with 10,000 credits a month, about 10 minutes of audio. |

## Step by step: ElevenLabs

1. Create a free account at **elevenlabs.io**.
2. Open **Text to Speech**.
3. Pick a voice from the **Voice Library**. Filter by Spanish and listen to several before choosing.
4. Paste your text, generate and download the audio.
5. Want **your own voice**? **Instant Voice Cloning** starts with the Starter plan, the cheapest one.

## Step by step: Google AI Studio

1. Go to **aistudio.google.com** with your Google account.
2. Open the speech generation option and pick a voice (TTS) model.
3. Choose a voice from the list.
4. Write **how** you want it said first, then the text. For example:

```
Say it excited, at a slow pace and with a warm tone, as if you were telling a friend a secret: "Today I'm bringing you three tools to create AI voices."
```

5. Listen, change the instruction (faster, more serious, whispering) and compare.

## Step by step: MiniMax Audio

1. Go to **minimax.io** and open the **Audio** section.
2. **Text to speech:** pick a voice and language, paste your text and generate.
3. **Clone your voice:** upload a recording of yourself of at least 10 seconds, with no music or background noise.
4. **Music:** describe the song you want (style, mood, instruments) and generate.

## Prompt to make your text sound natural (works in all 3)

```
Rewrite this text so it sounds natural when spoken out loud: short sentences, no parentheses or abbreviations, numbers written out in words and pauses marked with commas. Don't change the meaning. Text: [paste your text]
```

## Before you use it

- **Only clone your own voice** or a voice whose owner gave written permission.
- The ElevenLabs free plan **has no commercial license**: for ads or paid work you need a paid plan.
- Plans and limits change often. Data checked on November 26, 2026: confirm on each tool's pricing page.
