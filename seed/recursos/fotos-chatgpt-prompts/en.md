Five prompts to improve your photos with ChatGPT, ready to copy and paste: professional camera finish, light and shadows, clean background, natural colors and framing for social media. Below you download the PDF to read on your phone and the editable version (both in Spanish).

## How to use them

1. Open ChatGPT.
2. Attach the photo you want to improve.
3. Copy and paste the prompt you need.
4. Review the result before saving it.

## The five prompts

1. **Give it a professional camera finish**
2. **Fix the light and shadows**
3. **Clean up the background**
4. **Give it more natural colors**
5. **Improve the framing and finish**
