You send a sticker and the joke falls flat because it doesn't make a sound? **StikSound** is a free, open-source program (MIT or Apache-2.0 license) that joins your sticker with the sound of any meme, up to 5 or 10 seconds long. It came out on November 24, 2026, and by the next day it already had 138 stars on GitHub.

=> [Open StikSound on the web](https://silverfeit.github.io/StikSound/)

=> [See StikSound on GitHub](https://github.com/SilverFeit/StikSound)

## What it does, explained simply

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Your sticker | Takes PNG, JPG, WebP or animated GIF and crops it square (max 512 × 512) |
| The audio | MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A or AAC, or a MyInstants link |
| The trim | Pick a piece from 0.1 to 5 or 10 seconds with the **5s / 10s** button |
| The output | MP4, WebM with transparency, project ZIP or WebP without audio |
| Privacy | Everything is processed on your device: no server receives your files |

## Web or install it?

Start with the **web version**: nothing to install, and it works on PC, Android and iPhone.

| | Web | Desktop app or Chrome extension |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Install | No | Yes, from Releases |
| Audio you already downloaded | Yes | Yes |
| Paste MyInstants links | No (download the MP3 first) | Yes |
| Short video as sticker | Yes | No |
| WebM with transparency | No | Yes |

## Your sticker with sound, step by step

1. **Open StikSound** (web or app).
2. **Paste your sticker** with **Ctrl + V** (Cmd + V on Mac), drag it, or click the center area.
3. **Frame it:** if the image isn't square, move the mouse over it, without clicking.
4. **Add the audio** with the audio button, by dragging the file, or by pasting the MyInstants link (in the app or extension).
5. **Trim the piece:** tap the waveform, move the window and choose 5 or 10 seconds. The play button lets you hear the exact clip.
6. **Choose the background** (transparent, white, black or color) and tap **→** to copy it or **⬇** to download it.

## The meme sound, from MyInstants

1. Find the sound on [myinstants.com](https://www.myinstants.com) and open it.
2. **In the app or extension:** copy that page's address and paste it into StikSound.
3. **On the web:** tap "Download MP3" on MyInstants, then choose that file in StikSound.

MyInstants sounds belong to their authors: use them for fun with your friends, not to sell.

## Taking it to WhatsApp

> **The MP4 you get is a video, not a sticker.** If you paste it in a chat, it's sent as a video.

- **WhatsApp beta for Android:** on some accounts you can tap **Create sticker** and pick that video from your gallery. WhatsApp decides the length and audio, and it isn't officially documented.
- **Other versions:** send it as a short video; it looks and sounds the same in the chat.
- **If copy doesn't paste:** download it with ⬇ and share it manually.

## If you want the app

1. Go to the [official repo's Releases](https://github.com/SilverFeit/StikSound/releases/latest) and download only from there.
2. **Windows:** the `windows-x64` file. **Mac:** the `macos-universal` one; if you see the unverified developer warning, right-click the app and choose **Open**.
3. **Chrome, Edge or Brave:** unzip the extension ZIP into a permanent folder, open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode** and click **Load unpacked**.

## Keep in mind

- It's a **very new** project (version 1.1.1, November 25, 2026): it may change fast.
- It's independent: not an official WhatsApp or MyInstants app.
