## What exactly you get

- **Forage (9 of the 11):** free job simulations with tasks guided by video and example answers from each company. When you finish you get a **certificate of completion**. Forage suggests adding it to your résumé and LinkedIn as an **extracurricular activity**.
- **Kaggle:** free courses with a **certificate of completion** once you finish the exercises.
- **Simplilearn:** free course with a **completion certificate** and 90 days of access.

These certify that you completed a course or a simulation; they are **not official certifications** or work experience. BCG, for example, explicitly asks you not to list its simulation as a job or internship.

## How to start

1. Create your free account on [Forage](https://www.theforage.com), [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com) and [Simplilearn](https://www.simplilearn.com/skillup-free-online-courses).
2. Start with the short ones (Deloitte, Simplilearn, Mastercard, Vista) to build momentum.
3. Save the 3–4 hour ones for the weekend (BCG, Tata, Commonwealth Bank, Datacom, Bank of America, J.P. Morgan).
4. Download each certificate and add it on LinkedIn under **Licenses & certifications**, or to your résumé as an activity.

Simplilearn, Vista and Datacom have no clear related role, so they have no figure. A free certificate helps you learn and show initiative, but it **doesn't guarantee that job or that salary**.
