Three GitHub repositories to look at before paying for your next website: **FreeDomain** (free domains with other extensions), **Free for Dev** (services with a free plan) and **Awesome Selfhosted** (apps you can host yourself). Below you download the PDF for your phone and the editable version with the checklist (both in Spanish).

**Checked on October 31, 2026** in each repository's README and FAQ. The terms of each service change: confirm them at the source.

## What it includes

1. **FreeDomain (DigitalPlat):** look for an available name and review the conditions. It isn't the same as buying a .com.
2. **Free for Dev:** compare each plan's limits with what you need before choosing.
3. **Awesome Selfhosted:** you decide how to install and maintain each app. The software may be free, but the server and maintenance may cost money.
4. **Checklist** of seven points before you set up your website.

Before setting everything up, check each service's conditions and keep backups.
