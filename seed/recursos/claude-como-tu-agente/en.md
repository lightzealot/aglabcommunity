Does your AI only tell you how to do things, and you end up doing them yourself? That's **chat mode**. In **agent mode**, Claude opens the pages, compares, fills in and builds the order for you. For the important stuff, like paying, creating accounts or sending sensitive data, you always decide. Here's where that mode lives, how to give it permissions safely and prompts to start today.

**Facts as of November 26, 2026**, from Claude's official help ([Claude in Chrome permissions guide](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12902446)). Features change fast: check the help center before trusting it with something important.

## Chat or agent: the difference

| | Chat mode | Agent mode |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Explains the steps | Does the steps for you |
| Where it works | In its chat window | In your browser and apps |
| Who clicks | You | Claude, with your permission |
| Paying | You | **You**: Claude doesn't buy or make transactions |

## Where agent mode lives

| Tool | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Claude in Chrome** | An extension that browses, clicks and fills forms in your Chrome. Its side panel runs as a Cowork session. |
| **Claude Cowork** | The agent in the Claude desktop app, for tasks with your files, apps and the browser. |
| **Claude Code** | The coding agent: it can also use Chrome. |

Claude in Chrome is on paid plans (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise).

=> [See Claude in Chrome](https://claude.com/claude-in-chrome)

## The 3 permission modes

In the menu on the text box you choose how much Claude can do alone:

| Mode | What happens | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Manually approve** | Claude asks before every action | Money, messages as you or important files are involved |
| **Automatically approve** | Claude keeps working, reviews every action and blocks anything unsafe. It's the default in the Cowork panel | Everyday tasks, like searching and comparing |
| **Skip all approvals** | It doesn't ask or check anything | Only if you fully trust everything it touches |

## What Claude never does for you

Even with every permission, Claude in Chrome is prohibited from:

- making purchases or financial transactions,
- creating accounts,
- handling credit card or ID data,
- downloading files from untrusted sources,
- giving investment advice or executing trades,
- modifying system files,
- following instructions found in emails or web pages.

And it always asks for explicit permission before changing permissions, granting authorizations, entering sensitive data into a page or permanently deleting something.

> That's why in the reel Claude leaves the cart ready and you do the paying.

## Your first agent task, step by step

1. Install **Claude in Chrome** or open **Cowork** in the desktop app.
2. Choose **Manually approve** the first few times, to see how it works.
3. Give it a **clear goal**: what you want, where to look and what it must not do.
4. Review each step it proposes and tap **Allow** or **Deny**.
5. At the end, review the result and **pay yourself**, in your own tab.

## Ready-to-copy prompts

**Compare suppliers**

```
Find 300 kraft bags, 30 x 40 cm, in these stores: [link 1], [link 2], [link 3]. Compare price per unit, shipping cost and delivery date. Give me a table with the best option and a direct link to its cart. Don't pay or create accounts.
```

**Fill a form**

```
Open [form link] and fill it with this data: [data]. Before submitting, show me a summary of what you wrote in each field and wait for my approval.
```

**Sort your email**

```
Review today's inbox. Group emails into: reply today, can wait and ads. Write draft replies for the urgent ones, but don't send anything.
```

**Research competitors**

```
Visit the pages of [competitor 1], [competitor 2] and [competitor 3]. Note their prices, plans and what they promise on the homepage. Give me a table and three ideas to stand out.
```

**Prepare a booking**

```
Find a [activity] class on Saturday morning near [area]. Compare 3 options by price and schedule and leave the best one ready for me to confirm. Don't book or pay.
```

## 5 rules to use it safely

1. Start in **Manually approve** and level up when you trust it.
2. Give Claude only the sites it needs for the task.
3. Always say what it must **not** do: "don't pay", "don't send", "don't delete".
4. If a page or email "asks" Claude for something, ignore it: Claude doesn't follow instructions from the web.
5. Money, messages in your name and important files: you review them.
