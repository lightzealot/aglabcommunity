A short, visual guide to record yourself reading a script from your phone with **Teleprompter Studio**, a free web app: open the link, paste your script, tap the red button and read while looking at the lens. Nothing to install and no account needed. Below you download the PDF and the editable version (both in Spanish).

**App link:** [https://telepronterstudio.netlify.app](https://telepronterstudio.netlify.app)

## What it includes

1. **Prepare your recording:** open the link, accept permissions, paste the script and choose speed and text size.
2. **Record:** rest the phone at eye level, tap the red button and read while looking at the lens. How to pause with a tap and go back to the start.
3. **Save your video:** separate steps for iPhone and Android, and a warning so you don't lose your takes.
4. **Four tips to start:** speed 60 to 70, text size 40 to 44, short sentences and good light.

The phone diagrams in the PDF are supporting illustrations, not screenshots of the app.
