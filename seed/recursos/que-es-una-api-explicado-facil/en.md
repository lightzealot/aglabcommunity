They say "API", "backend" or "deploy" and you nod without understanding? Relax: nobody is born knowing. Here are **10 programming words explained like you're 5**, each with a real-life example and a prompt so Claude explains it with your own project.

## The pizzeria from the reel, in a table

| At the pizzeria | In an app | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Your table and the menu | **Frontend** | What you see and touch: screens, buttons, text |
| The waiter | **API** | Takes your order and brings back the answer |
| The kitchen | **Backend** | Where everything is prepared and stored |
| You get pineapple but ordered pepperoni | **Bug** | An error: you asked for one thing and got another |
| "No more orders today" | **Rate limit** | A cap on orders per minute or per day |

## The 10 words

| # | Word | Explained like you're 5 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **API** | The waiter: you say what you want, they go to the kitchen and bring it. You never enter the kitchen. |
| 2 | **Frontend** | The table, the menu and the decor: everything you see of the app. |
| 3 | **Backend** | The kitchen: nobody sees it, but the work happens there. |
| 4 | **Database** | The pantry with everything sorted on shelves: users, orders, prices. |
| 5 | **Server** | The building where the kitchen is, on day and night. |
| 6 | **Bug** | You ordered pepperoni and got pineapple. Something came out different from what was asked. |
| 7 | **Deploy** | Opening the pizzeria to the public with the new menu. |
| 8 | **Git** | A notebook where you write down every change to the recipe, so you can go back to an older version. |
| 9 | **Rate limit** | "Max 3 candies a day": the app only accepts a certain number of orders. |
| 10 | **Token** | Each little piece of text the AI reads or writes, like the chips in a game. |
