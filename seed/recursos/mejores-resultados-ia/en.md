title: A template to explain your task better to an AI
summary: Explain your situation, define the goal and ask for the format you need.
---
By Andrés Gómez · @andresgomez.ia

You don't need magic words or complicated instructions.
AI answers better when it understands three things:
- Situation: what's going on?
- Goal: what do you need to achieve?
- Format: how do you want to receive the answer?

## Main template · copy, fill in and paste

```text
This is my situation:
[Briefly explain what's going on and include the important details].

I need to achieve:
[Clearly describe the result you expect].

I want the answer to be:
[State the format, tone, length and level of difficulty].

Before answering, if you're missing an important detail, ask me the
questions you need.
```

## Quick version

```text
Context: [What's going on?]
Goal:    [What do you need to achieve?]
Format:  [How do you want to receive the answer?]
```

## Examples

### Write an email

Basic instruction:
"Write me an email."

Better instruction:
"A client hasn't replied to a proposal I sent them five days ago.
Write a short, friendly and professional follow-up email.
I don't want it to sound pushy and it should end with a clear question."

### Create content

"I have an Instagram account where I teach artificial intelligence to
people who are just starting. Give me five Reel ideas that are easy to
film. For each one include a short hook, the main lesson and
a CTA. Use simple language and avoid jargon."

### Learn a topic

"I'm starting to learn artificial intelligence and I still don't
understand what a language model is. Explain it in simple words,
using an everyday comparison and a practical example.
Keep the answer to five paragraphs."

### Organize a task

"I have to prepare a ten-minute presentation to explain a
business idea. Help me organize it into five sections. For each
section, state the main message and what I should show on
screen."

## If the first answer doesn't convince you

You don't need to start another conversation. Follow up with phrases like:

- Make it shorter and more direct.
- Explain it in simpler words.
- Give me an example applied to my case.
- Change the tone so it sounds warmer.
- Organize it in steps.
- Give me three different options.
- Remove the repetition.
- What information are you missing to improve the answer?
- Review your answer and tell me what could be wrong or incomplete.

## Checklist

Before sending your instruction, check:

- I explained the situation.
- I said exactly what I need.
- I stated how I want to receive the answer.
- I included the details that can change the result.
- I avoided sharing private or confidential information.

## Important

Don't share passwords, ID numbers, bank details,
sensitive medical information, business secrets or private
information about other people.

AI can make mistakes. Check names, numbers, dates and important
decisions before using the answer.

## Try it

Try the template with a task from today and adjust the context if the first answer doesn't help.
