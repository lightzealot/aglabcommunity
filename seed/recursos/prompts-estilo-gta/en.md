Your photo doesn't turn out the way you imagined? Here are the **5 GTA-style prompts** from the carousel, ready to copy, and the **tweak to keep your face**, which makes the character still look like you. Below you download the PDF for your phone and the prompts in an editable file (both in Spanish).

## How to use them

1. Open ChatGPT or Gemini (any that can create images from a photo).
2. Attach a photo of yourself: facing the camera, with good light and your face clearly visible.
3. Paste the keep-your-face tweak first and, below it, the prompt for the style you want.
4. If the face doesn't look like you, paste the fix below into the same chat.

## The tweak to keep your face

Paste it **before** any of the 5 prompts. It's what stops the AI from changing your face.

```
Use the photo I attach as the only identity reference. Keep the shape of the face, the eyes, the nose, the mouth, the eyebrows, the hairline, the beard, the skin tone and the age exactly the same. Don't beautify or change the features: change only the style, clothes and background I ask for. If you have to choose, prioritize likeness over style.
```

If someone else still comes out, paste this into the same chat:

```
The face doesn't look like the one in the photo. Generate the image again with the same style and the same scene, but copy exactly the features of the person in the original photo.
```

## The 5 GTA-style prompts

### 1. Loading screen

```
Transform the person in the photo into a GTA-style loading screen illustration. Keep their face, hair, beard and features. Waist-up portrait, black leather jacket and dark sunglasses worn low, with the eyes visible. Black outlines and cel shading. Pink and orange sky, skyline and Ferris wheel in the background. 80s synthwave colors. No text or logos.
```

### 2. Yacht day

```
Place the person in the photo on the back deck of a yacht in a Miami bay. Keep their face, hair and beard. Sitting on cream leather, open linen shirt, gold chain and aviator sunglasses; raising a cold drink. Turquoise water, white wake, Art Deco hotels, palm trees and speedboats. Afternoon sun, realistic cinematic photography. No text or logos.
```

### 3. Sunset poster

```
Turn this photo into an action video game poster in GTA style. Keep face, hair, beard, age, pose and clothes. Semi-realistic painted finish, clean outlines, soft shadows and strong highlights. Pink and orange sunset, purple and turquoise reflections. Behind, a coastal street with palm trees, a luxury sports car and distant neon lights. No text or logos.
```

### 4. Muscle car

```
Redraw the person in the photo as a GTA-style cover character. Keep face, hair, beard and clothes. Knee-up framing, leaning on a classic muscle car on a wet street at dusk. Palm trees and pastel Art Deco buildings with neon lights in the background. Clean outlines, cel shading and subtle halftone. Pink and orange sky. No text or logos.
```

### 5. Five panels

```
Transform the person in the photo into a five-panel collage in the style of a GTA loading screen, separated by black borders. At the top, their face in close-up with sunglasses. In the others: a sports car on a road at sunset, a briefcase of cash, palm trees under a purple sky and the same person leaning on the car. Keep their identity. Cel shading, pink, orange and purple. No text or logos.
```

## Tips to make it look like you

- A front or three-quarter photo, with no filters and no dark sunglasses.
- The face should take up a good part of the photo (not a far-away full-body shot).
- Only one person in the photo.
- Ask for one style per message; if you mix several, the likeness is lost.
