---
title: A 60-minute system to create a week of content with AI
summary: From one idea to Reels, a carousel, stories and a weekly calendar ready to produce.
---
Turn **one idea** into five organized pieces: 2 Reels, 1 carousel, 2 story sequences, 5 captions and a weekly calendar. The 60 minutes are a way to structure the session; filming, designing and editing take extra time.

**Before you start:** a conversational AI, a document to save the answers, a description of your audience and a specific problem you know how to solve. Don't ask for "content ideas": start from a problem.

## Min 0–10: the core idea

Fill in: *This week I'll help **[person]** get **[result]** without **[obstacle]**.*

```text
Act as an Instagram content strategist.
Audience: [who you help]. Topic: [topic]. Problem: [specific problem].
Suggest 5 angles for one week. Each one: a clear result, no exaggeration, can become Reels, a carousel and stories, in one sentence. Recommend the best one and explain why.
```

Pick the angle that solves a recognizable problem, that you can back with your experience and that leads to your product or resource.

## Min 10–25: five pieces

| Day | Format | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Reel | Grab attention: the common mistake |
| Tuesday | Stories | Start a conversation: poll |
| Wednesday | Carousel | Teach the method step by step |
| Thursday | Stories | Handle an objection |
| Friday | Reel | Show the result |

```text
Turn this idea into a week of Instagram content.
IDEA: [idea]. AUDIENCE: [audience]. GOAL: [educate / start conversations / capture leads / sell].
Create: 2 Reel ideas, 1 six-slide carousel and 2 sequences of 3 stories. For each piece: goal, hook, main idea, CTA and how it connects to the next one. Don't repeat the same information. Don't make up data.
```

## Min 25–40: drafts

```text
Script for a 30–45 s Reel about [topic]. Audience: [audience]. Tone: [tone].
Structure: one-sentence hook, problem, practical points, short example and one CTA. Short sentences that are easy to say. No clichés or absolute promises. Include the on-screen text.
```

```text
Six-slide carousel about [topic]: cover with a promise, context, 3 concrete steps and CTA. 25 words max per slide and one visual suggestion per slide. One idea per screen.
```

```text
Sequence of 3 stories about [topic]: 1) a situation the audience recognizes, 2) a short tip, 3) interaction (poll or question box). Exact on-screen text, 18 words max per story.
```

## Min 40–50: captions and CTA

```text
Instagram caption for: [script or summary].
A first line that sparks curiosity, 2–4 short paragraphs, a clear conclusion and a single CTA: [save / comment / share / keyword].
Tone: [tone]. No generic phrases, too many emojis or claims that can't be checked.
```

CTA by goal: **reach** ("share it with someone who…"), **saves**, **conversation** ("which part takes up most of your time?"), **resource** ("comment WEEK and I'll send you the guide").

## Min 50–60: calendar and production

Working table: Day | Piece | Hook | CTA | What's left to produce | Status (**idea → draft → review → produced → scheduled → published**).

```text
With this calendar: [paste the five pieces], create a production list grouped by type of task (film, gather assets, design, edit, schedule) and order it so I can work in batches. Mark what I need to check before publishing.
```

## Final review: make it sound like you

```text
Review this draft as an editor: [draft].
My voice: [3 traits]. Expressions I use: [examples]. That I don't use: [examples].
Remove clichés, shorten lines that are hard to say, flag claims to verify and point out where to add an experience of mine. First the notes; then the revised version without inventing experiences for me.
```

## Checklist before publishing

- [ ] The hook promises what the piece delivers.
- [ ] There's an example, opinion or personal experience.
- [ ] I checked names, numbers and dates.
- [ ] Each piece has a single CTA.
- [ ] The promised resource exists and is ready to send.

**To repeat it:** keep the structure, change only the core idea, reuse your audience's real questions and set aside another session to film and design in batches.
