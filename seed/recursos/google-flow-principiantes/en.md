**Google Flow** is an AI studio to create images and video clips, reuse characters or objects as references and build sequences with Scenebuilder.

**Access:** it works with usage limits and credits, and depends on your country, account and plan. There are trial options and paid plans; extra credits may cost money. It's not completely free: check your credits and what each model uses before generating.

## Your first project

1. Go to https://flow.google.com/ with your Google account and create a project.
2. Choose image or video and describe the scene.
3. Add references if you need to keep a character, product or style.
4. Choose orientation, length and number of results; generate and review.
5. **Change only one variable per attempt** (camera, light, action, style or composition).
6. Save the best ones and arrange the clips in Scenebuilder before downloading.

## Prompt formula

**Subject + action + place + shot + camera movement + lighting + style**

*Example:* "A creator working at a laptop in a modern studio, medium shot, the camera slowly moves in, blue and orange lighting, realistic cinematic style."

## 3 prompts to try

```text
Editorial portrait of a tech creator at a minimalist desk, confident and natural expression, soft side lighting, dark background with blue details, professional photography, vertical composition.
```

```text
A smartphone on a black table, studio lights sweep along its edges while the camera slowly orbits the product, controlled reflections, dark background, premium advertising look.
```

```text
A creator works on a laptop inside an airplane. The walls of the plane disappear and they're left sitting above the clouds. Medium shot, camera slowly moving in, warm sunrise light, realistic cinematic style.
```

## Improve without starting over

- **Bad framing:** ask for a close-up, medium shot or wide shot.
- **Too much movement:** static camera or slow movement.
- **The character changes:** use the same reference and describe what must stay the same.
- **It looks artificial:** natural light, realistic texture, coherent motion.
- **It doesn't do the action:** a single action, in order, with no contradictory instructions.

**Common mistakes:** very general prompts, many actions in one clip, changing everything on each attempt, not using references and burning credits repeating without analyzing what went wrong.

**Exercise:** a 5–8 s idea → prompt with the formula → first version → change only one thing → compare.
