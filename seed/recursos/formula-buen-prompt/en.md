title: The formula for a good prompt
summary: Role, context, task, format and example: a simple formula and five ready-to-copy prompts you can adapt.
---
**Role + Context + Task + Format + Example**

## The 5 ingredients

### 1. Role: who the AI is

> Act as a digital marketing expert.

### 2. Context: for whom and why

> I'm talking to entrepreneurs who are just starting on Instagram.

### 3. Task: exactly what it should do

> Write 3 hooks for a carousel about AI.

### 4. Format: how you want it

> 10 words max each, as a list.

### 5. Example: something you like

> In a style similar to: "It's not that AI is bad…"

## Bonus: leave room for questions

If missing details would change the answer, add:

> Before answering, ask only for the information you need.

## 5 ready-to-copy prompts

Replace whatever is in **[brackets]** with your own information.

### 1. Content

```text
Act as an Instagram content strategist. I'm talking to [your audience] and I want [goal]. Write 5 carousel ideas about [topic], each with a hook of 10 words max, as a numbered list. Style: direct, practical and no fluff. Before answering, ask only for the information you need.
```

### 2. Emails

```text
Act as an executive assistant with good judgment. Write an email to [recipient] about [subject]. Tone: [friendly / firm / warm]. 120 words max, with a clear subject line and a single action at the end. Give me two versions: a short one and a full one.
```

### 3. Ideas

```text
Act as a creative consultant. I need ideas for [project or problem]. My context: [budget, time, resources]. Give me 10 ideas in a table with: idea, effort (low/medium/high) and expected impact. Mark the 3 you recommend and explain why in one line each.
```

### 4. Summary

```text
Act as an analyst who summarizes for busy people. Summarize the following text in 5 key points, followed by 3 decisions or actions that come out of it. Then tell me what information is missing or questionable. Text: [paste the text here].
```

### 5. Study

```text
Act as a patient tutor. I want to learn [topic] and my level is [beginner / intermediate]. Explain it with an everyday analogy, then with a step-by-step example, and finish with 5 practice questions. Hold the answers until I ask; then show them at the end.
```

---

## Golden rule

If the result is generic, **add context, make the task specific and show an example of what you expect**.
