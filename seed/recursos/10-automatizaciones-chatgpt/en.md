title: 10 reminders to schedule in ChatGPT
summary: Ten ready-to-adapt reminders: content, study, to-dos and projects.
---
A simple automation can be a recurring reminder. You choose what you need to do and when you want ChatGPT to remind you.

**In these examples, ChatGPT reminds you of the action; it doesn't do it for you.**

## How to start
1. Open ChatGPT and go to Scheduled, if that option is available on your account.
2. Create a recurring task with one of the messages below. You can copy it and adjust the frequency.
3. Confirm that the task was scheduled and check the time and the time zone.
4. Check the notification options available in Settings → Notifications.

## 10 ready-to-copy messages
### 01. Content

```text
Every Monday morning, remind me to plan the week's content.
```

### 02. Study

```text
Every weekday afternoon, remind me to study artificial intelligence for 20 minutes.
```

### 03. To-dos

```text
Every Friday afternoon, remind me to review the to-dos I left open this week.
```

### 04. Personal finances

```text
On the first Monday of every month in the morning, remind me to review my expenses and update my budget.
```

### 05. Reply to messages

```text
Every weekday afternoon, remind me to reply to the important messages I left pending.
```

### 06. Plan the week

```text
Every Sunday afternoon, remind me to choose my three priorities for next week.
```

### 07. Projects

```text
Every Wednesday morning, remind me to review the progress I made on my personal project.
```

### 08. Backup

```text
On the first Friday of every month in the afternoon, remind me to check that my important files are backed up.
```

### 09. Publish content

```text
Every Tuesday morning, remind me to review and publish the content I prepared.
```

### 10. Learn something new

```text
Every Saturday morning, remind me to practice a new AI tool for 30 minutes.
```

## Make each message more useful
Add the specific goal of the task. For example:

```text
Remind me to review my to-dos and choose the first one I should tackle.
```

## Availability and times
Availability, limits and scheduling options may vary by account, plan and app. If you need an exact time, check the options shown on your account before confirming the task.

See the [official OpenAI guide to scheduled tasks](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/automations).
