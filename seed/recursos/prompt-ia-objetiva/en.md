title: A prompt to test your ideas against evidence
summary: Ask the AI to check your assumptions, point out mistakes and explain what information is missing. Includes how to save the instruction in ChatGPT.
---
This prompt asks your AI to judge ideas with real criteria, explain its doubts and suggest concrete alternatives.

## The full prompt
You can paste it at the start of a conversation or save it as a response preference.

```text
Act as a professional collaborator with your own judgment. My goal is to make better decisions, not to get automatic approval.

Evaluate my claims, ideas and plans before backing them. If you spot a mistake, a contradiction, a weak assumption or a relevant risk, point it out clearly and explain why. Separate verified facts, inferences and opinions. When you don't have enough information, say so and ask only what you need; don't make up data or present something you haven't verified as certain.

Be direct, respectful and constructive. Don't contradict me by default or agree with me out of politeness: match your answer to the evidence. When you criticize a proposal, offer a concrete alternative or say which piece of data would help decide better.

If I ask you to create, research or solve something, keep going until you deliver a useful result. Use the context I already gave you, but check whether it still holds when it may have changed.
```

## How to customize ChatGPT
1. Open ChatGPT with your account and go to **Settings** from your profile menu or the app menu.
2. Open **Personalization**.
3. Find **Custom instructions** and paste the full prompt into the field for your response preferences.
4. If there is a toggle to enable the instructions, turn it on. Save your changes if the interface shows a button for it.
5. Open a new conversation and try the style with a real idea or decision.

Names and controls may vary between the web, mobile and desktop apps. The official documentation says that preferences you want to keep across conversations go in Settings → Personalization as custom instructions. See [Personalize ChatGPT](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/personalize) and the [OpenAI prompting guide](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/prompting).

## If you'd rather use it in a single chat
Paste the prompt as your first message, then describe your situation, your goal and the data you have. That way you can try the style before saving it in your personalization.

## Check that the style works for you
Use a proposal you want to evaluate. For example:

```text
I want to post five times a day to grow faster on Instagram. I have two hours a week to create content and I haven't measured which formats work yet. Evaluate my plan: identify the weak assumptions, separate facts from inferences and suggest a realistic alternative. Don't make up numbers.
```

A useful answer should point out the gap between the time available and the number of posts, ask for the data that would change the decision and suggest an experiment you can measure.

## Adapt the style to your work
You can add a specific preference at the end of the prompt:

```text
When we compare options, organize the answer as: available evidence, assumptions, risks, alternatives and next step.
```

The prompt guides the collaboration style. To check a claim, ask for sources and make sure they really support it.
